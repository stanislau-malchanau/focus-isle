# Focus Isle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-16

This policy explains how Focus Isle (the "App"), made by Stanislau Malchanau ("we", "us"),
handles your information. Short version: **we do not collect, sell or share your personal
data. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.**

## 1. Data the App stores

The App stores the following on your device: your focus sessions (start, end, subject and
result), subjects, day tasks and goals, settings, the islands you have reached, the finds
you have collected, your captain's rank and your boat's look.

We do not run servers that receive this data, and we have no access to it. The
exceptions are the timing of a running Live Activity (section 6) and anonymous usage
signals (section 9).

## 2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit, in the
private database of your own iCloud account. This data is stored and protected by Apple
under Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot see, read or
export it. You can turn sync off in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

## 3. Location and weather

To show the real weather and daylight over the island, the App asks for your approximate
location, only while it is open, and only if you allow it. The location is rounded to
about one kilometre and sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the current weather and
the times of sunrise and sunset. We never receive your location. The App keeps the last
weather answer on your device. Without permission the App follows your phone's clock
instead. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location
Services > Focus Isle. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

## 4. Headphone motion and spatial sound

With AirPods or other supported headphones, the App can use the movement of your head to
keep the island's sounds in place as you turn. This motion data is processed on your device
in real time, is never stored and never leaves your device.

## 5. Strict mode and Screen Time

If you turn on strict mode, the App uses Apple's Screen Time framework to shield the apps
you choose during a focus session. Apple gives the App only opaque tokens for those apps,
which stay on your device; we cannot see which apps you chose or how you use them.

## 6. Notifications and Live Activity

Reminders, such as the reminder before a free trial ends, are created on your device.

While a focus session runs, the App can show a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the
Dynamic Island. So that it keeps moving while the App is asleep, the App sends our small
relay server the Live Activity's push token from Apple and the session's schedule: when each
voyage and break starts and ends, the island number, the activity colour and today's count
against your daily goal. No name, email, subject, account or device identifier is sent. At
each moment of the schedule the server asks Apple's push service to update the Live
Activity, and it deletes everything as soon as the Live Activity ends. The server runs on
Cloudflare Workers; like any internet service, Cloudflare sees the IP address of the request
while delivering it, and we do not store it. If you turn Live Activities off for the App in
iOS Settings, nothing is sent.

## 7. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements are reported to Apple's Game Center
and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center identity.

## 8. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive
your payment details. Apple tells the App only whether your subscription is active.

## 9. Usage analytics and crash reports

To learn which features help and where the App gets in the way, the App sends anonymous
usage signals to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service of TelemetryDeck GmbH (Augsburg,
Germany) with servers in the EU. A signal names an action in the App: for example, that a
focus session started, finished or was given up, with its length and mode; that the day's
goal was met or changed; that strict mode was turned on; that the subscription screen was
shown or a subscription started; or that iCloud sync failed. It comes with a timestamp
rounded to the hour and general technical details: the App and iOS versions, the device
model, the language, region and time zone, and display and accessibility settings such as
dark mode or text size.

A signal never contains your subjects, tasks or any other text you enter, your name, email
address, Apple Account or location. Your installation is identified only by a random
identifier that the App salts and hashes before sending, so the signals cannot be traced
back to you or followed across apps. TelemetryDeck stores no IP addresses and uses no
cookies. We look at these signals only as statistics to improve the App; we do not sell
them, share them with advertisers or use them to track you. The App contains no
advertising or tracking code and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Separately, if you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings > Privacy
& Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage
statistics and crash reports. They do not identify you.

## 10. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from
anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## 11. Data retention and deletion

Data stays as long as you keep it. You can delete it at any time by deleting the App and,
for synced data, in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Focus
Isle. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further for us to delete. The relay server
keeps a Live Activity's schedule only until that Live Activity ends. Usage signals are
anonymous and cannot be matched to you, so there is no personal record of them to look up
or delete.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA in
California, APPI in Japan, PIPA in Korea or LGPD in Brazil), you may have the right to
access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its
processing. Since we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights directly on your
device and in iCloud as described above. You can also contact us with any request or
question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes,
tell you in the App.
